## Judge check (12 Nov 2026): a second judge on a stratified sample

The Lab's assigned audit drew 64 judged diagnoses (12 per R3 configuration, 4 from the earlier `R3 B2 +ROUTING`
label, 12 pooled from round 2; half judge-pass, half judge-fail per stratum) and re-judged them with `openai/gpt-5.4`
through SREGym's own judge (same reference answer, only the judge model changes).

- Agreement 56 of 64 = 88% (Wilson 77% to 94%), Cohen's kappa 0.75. Original pass rate on the sample 31/64, GPT-5.4 23/64.
- All 8 disagreements are original pass, GPT-5.4 fail: storage_user_unregistered-2 (4), psa_restricted_blocks_recreation (3),
  expired_tls (1). Round 2 pooled: 12 of 12 agree.
- By stratum: F 11/12, R(+FLAT) 9/12, P 10/12, PR 11/12, old R 3/4.
- Reading: our judge is the more lenient one, so reported diagnosis rates may be optimistic. Which judge is right on the
  8 is not settled; a person reading those 8 against the cards would settle it. The sample is too small to say whether
  the leniency favours one configuration.
